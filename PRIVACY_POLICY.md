# Lumina Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Lumina is designed with a privacy-first approach. All screenshot capturing, cropping, filtering, and annotation processes happen **completely locally on your device**. 

Lumina **does not** track, collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data, browsing history, web pages, or the images you capture.

## 2. Chrome Extension Permissions Explained
To function correctly, Lumina requires the following permissions, which are used strictly for local operation:
- **`activeTab` & `debugger`**: Used exclusively to attach to the current tab and capture a high-resolution, full-page scrolling screenshot when you explicitly click the extension icon. No screenshot data is ever sent to external servers.
- **`downloads`**: Used strictly to allow you to save your finalized PNG or PDF files to your local hard drive.
- **`storage` / `unlimitedStorage`**: Used to temporarily pass the massive base64 image data of the screenshot from the background capture engine to the local editor window.

## 3. "Report an Issue" Feature
If you voluntarily choose to use the "Report an Issue" feature within the extension, the information you provide (such as your email address, browser user-agent, and message) is securely transmitted directly to the developer via the EmailJS API. 
This data is used **solely for the purpose of customer support and bug fixing**, and is never shared with, sold to, or marketed by third parties.

## 4. Third-Party Integrations
Lumina utilizes the EmailJS REST API strictly for the transmission of voluntary issue reports. No other third-party analytics, trackers, or data collection scripts are included in this extension.
